01 / Adopt

Find a companion who fits your life.

Start with a conversation about your home, routine, and hopes for a new companion. Ask our team about current animals and their individual needs.

  1. Tell us about the companion you’re looking for.
  2. Discuss care needs and arrange a meeting with the team.
  3. Prepare your home and ask about the next adoption steps.

02 / Foster

Be their safe place.

Offer a temporary home while a pet waits for a permanent family. Contact us to discuss your space, availability, and the support you would need. Fosters must be at least 18 years old, have permission to keep animals in their home, and complete an orientation and home-safety review. Typical placements last two to eight weeks. The rescue provides food, basic supplies, approved veterinary care, and weekly check-ins; fosters provide daily care and updates.

03 / Volunteer

Put your kindness to work.

Animal care, socialization, and community support all begin with willing hands. Ask about opportunities that match your interests and schedule.

Volunteer roles to ask about

  • Animal care: help with feeding, cleaning, and daily comfort under team guidance.
  • Socialization: spend supervised time helping animals feel comfortable around people.
  • Community outreach: help share rescue information and support awareness activities.

Animal care and socialization shifts are available Tuesday through Sunday, and community outreach opportunities are available on selected weekends. Volunteers must be at least 18 years old and complete a one-hour orientation. Animal care volunteers also complete two supervised training shifts before working independently.
