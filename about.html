Our Organization Story

In this fictional classroom story, Twin Cities Animal Rescue began in 2018 when six neighbors organized a foster network to help animals find safe homes. The group opened a small community rescue office in 2020 and launched its volunteer orientation program in 2022. By the end of 2025, the rescue had grown to 65 active foster homes, united by a shared commitment to compassionate care.

We believe choosing a companion begins with understanding an animal’s needs and the care a household can provide. Thoughtful conversations help people make informed commitments.

Our Core Values

Compassion
Treat animals and people with patience and respect.
Responsible care
Encourage thoughtful placements and informed commitments.
Community
Welcome volunteers, fosters, donors, and partners.
Trust
Communicate clearly about needs and opportunities.

These values guide how we care for animals, support volunteers, and work with our community.
